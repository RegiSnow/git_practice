02 — MY STORY

From Nursing to Building Integrated Healthcare

A professional journey shaped by clinical experience, systems thinking, entrepreneurship and technology.

My professional journey began in nursing. Clinical practice gave me a firsthand understanding of the responsibility involved in caring for people during vulnerable moments of their lives.

Nursing also exposed me to the realities of healthcare systems. I saw how communication gaps, fragmented workflows, limited resources, administrative inefficiencies and disconnected services could affect both healthcare professionals and patients.

Those experiences gradually changed the questions I was asking. Instead of only asking how I could provide better care to individual patients, I began asking how healthcare organizations could be designed to consistently enable better care.

This became the foundation of my systems-thinking approach. I became increasingly interested in clinical governance, quality management, healthcare operations, organizational strategy and technology.

This thinking ultimately led to the creation of Nivexia Integrated Healthcare Ltd., an organization built around the idea that healthcare should be coordinated rather than fragmented.

My technology journey developed from the same philosophy. As healthcare becomes increasingly dependent on digital infrastructure, cloud platforms, automation and data, I began expanding my expertise into DevOps, cloud engineering and healthcare analytics.

I am therefore not pursuing technology as a departure from healthcare. I am developing technology expertise because I believe future healthcare leaders must understand both the systems delivering care and the infrastructure powering them.

20 — FUTURE VISION

Building Africa's Next Generation of Healthcare Systems

My long-term objective is to contribute to the development of healthcare systems across Africa that are accessible, coordinated, financially sustainable and digitally enabled.
